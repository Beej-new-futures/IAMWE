James Harford & Daljit Kaur
About

Two practices, one name.

IAMWE is James Harford and Daljit Kaur — a motion designer, digital artist and filmmaker working alongside an interior and space designer, under a single studio name rather than two separate ones. The site itself is built the same way: a piece of digital artwork in its own right, that also happens to be where both practices show their work.

James Harford

Beej

Motion design · Digital art · Film

Motion designer, digital artist and filmmaker, across stage visuals, exhibition design and broadcast film. Recent work includes stage content for Drake and Dua Lipa, Our Story with David Attenborough at the Natural History Museum, and projection design for Gucci Cosmos in Kyoto.

Drake · Dua Lipa · David Attenborough · Gucci · FIFA World Cup · AC Milan · Feid

Daljit Kaur

Dal

Interior & space design · Ceramicist

Interior and space designer, artist and ceramicist, working on window schemes and public installations — most recently a run of campaigns for Selfridges, London, each built around a single idea and carried through to a physical set.

Selfridges · Music Matters · State of the Arts · Material World · The New Order
