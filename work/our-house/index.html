IW-02 — Daljit Kaur

Our House

A homeware scheme built on the idea that a house is really a collection of its objects. Each window is a room turned inside out — a timber frame hung with the entire contents of a kitchen, a wall of stacked earthenware standing over mounds of spice, a doorway spilling everything it holds onto the floor.
