Dua Lipa

Stage content for Dua Lipa's live show, made with Charli Davis. Three songs, each given its own visual language — One Kiss, Break My Heart and Falling Forever. The work plays across the tour's curved screen, so every frame had to hold as an environment wrapped around an arena rather than a picture seen flat.
