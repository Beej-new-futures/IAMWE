A Story About Bread

An eight-minute climate film produced by flour brand Wildfarmed, directed by James Payne and Beej&Them, with Beej on CGI and full post-production. A larger-than-life loaf hurtles towards Earth from space and lands in a 90s acid house scene, then the film runs through the Great Fire of London, the French Revolution and the bleach and emulsifiers in the everyday loaf, asking whether our appetite for bread is killing the planet when, with a few changes, it could be healing it.
