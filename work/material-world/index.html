Material World

A scheme about what clothes are made of, and what that costs. Each window takes a single material and builds the whole display out of it — cotton, wool, viscose, leather, plastic — with the campaign's puns lettered across the glass: life in plastic, not so fantastic; no wool pulled over our eyes.
