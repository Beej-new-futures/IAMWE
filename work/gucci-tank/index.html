IW-04 — Daljit Kaur

Gucci x Tank Magazine

A sculptural play of light, shadow and texture — raw cardboard forms fold, frame and interrupt the space, creating a landscape for the Gucci bags to inhabit. Rough, tactile surfaces meet glossy leather and hardware, turning contrast into the hero.
