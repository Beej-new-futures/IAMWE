Together We Rise

A four-part documentary on GRM Daily, the modern success story of Black British music, with contributions from Stormzy, Aitch, Dizzee Rascal, Ms. Banks and more. Beej&Them delivered the main title sequence and all of the show's graphics and visual effects; Beej directed and animated the documentary's animated stories, with James Cheetham and John Summerson.
