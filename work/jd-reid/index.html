JD Reid — Just Know

The first music video from JD Reid's album Tree, conceived and directed by Beej and Jeremy Cole. It takes the Voyager Golden Record as its starting point and sends JD's album into space instead, scattering the music like gold seeds that grow into trees deep in the cosmos, waiting for future civilisations to find. The whole film is one continuous camera move through the passage of time.
