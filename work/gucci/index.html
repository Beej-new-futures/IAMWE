Gucci Cosmos

Projection design and animation for Gucci Cosmos in Kyoto — an exhibition by Es Devlin, with video design by Luke Halls. Beej designed and animated the projected content that runs across the architecture of the show, so the room itself carries the archive rather than the walls simply holding it.
