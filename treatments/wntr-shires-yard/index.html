WINTER

26 Nov — 13 Dec 2026 Four acts of light 35 minutes Fifty people a showing

An immersive projection artwork in the heart of Bath — five surfaces, mapped edge to edge. From frost to flame.

Scroll — the show begins
Act I

Frost

Ice as living mathematics. Crystals bloom across five walls in macro — slow, intricate, impossibly close. The cold you walked in from, made beautiful.

Act II

Snowfield

A million particles in murmuration. Snow that swells, breathes and turns around you — weather as choreography, wrapping the room in silence.

Act III

Aurora

Liquid light, folded like silk. The northern sky brought indoors and slowed until it pours down the walls around you.

Act IV

Sea of Flames

A thousand candles that answer you. Reach out and the flames lean toward your hand; move, and embers lift from the walls. The room ends warm.

No story. No narrator. No Santa.
Just midwinter, made of light.

The Space/ 01

5 surfaces
Four walls and the ceiling, projection-mapped edge to edge. You stand inside the artwork.
35 minutes
A 25-minute film in four acts, followed by a 10-minute open interval — free to roam, touch and photograph as the flames respond to you.
50 people
Every showing is limited to fifty. Intimate by design — no crowds, no queues inside.
